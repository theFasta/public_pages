DPF Guard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

DPF Guard ("the App") is an Android application that reads live OBD2 data from a vehicle's engine control unit via a Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) dongle, and displays Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF/FAP) diagnostics on screen.

1. Data We Collect

The App collects no personal data. Specifically:

  • Bluetooth scan results — device names and signal strengths are shown during scanning and are never stored or transmitted outside the device.
  • OBD2 / vehicle readings — live values (soot mass, temperatures, pressures, etc.) are displayed on screen only and are never stored, logged, or transmitted.
  • Trial session timer — a timestamp is stored locally on the device (Android SharedPreferences) to track the free trial period. This value never leaves the device.
  • Purchase status — if you unlock the full version via in-app purchase, the entitlement status is stored locally on the device. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by the Google Play Store; we do not receive or store any payment information.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No user accounts or registration
  • No analytics or crash reporting services
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No location data (Bluetooth scan permission on Android ≤ 11 requires location permission at the OS level, but the App never reads or uses your GPS location)

3. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (Android 12+) — required to discover and connect to the OBD2 BLE dongle.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (Android ≤ 11 only) — required by the Android OS to perform BLE scans; no location data is accessed or stored by the App.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) — used to show a background monitoring notification and a regeneration alert when the app is not in the foreground.

4. Third-Party Services

In-app purchases are processed by the Google Play Store. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how purchase data is handled: policies.google.com/privacy .

The App does not integrate any other third-party SDK that collects data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored by the App (trial timer, purchase status) resides exclusively on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data in Android Settings → Apps → DPF Guard → Clear Data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
