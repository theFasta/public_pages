Ippo 一歩 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2025

Ippo is a personal Japanese study tool developed by Andrea Fastame. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data collection

Ippo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network requests.

Data stored on your device

All study content (flashcards, notes, preferences) is stored locally on your device using standard Android storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Ippo does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks.

Children

Ippo does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way (e.g. if a sync feature is added), this page will be updated with a new date and a clear description of what changed.
